What Is HBX?

Blues Dance comes to Hanoi

The Hanoi Blues Experience (HBX) is a weekend blues dance event built around community, connection, and the deep feeling of the blues. Set against the backdrop of one of Southeast Asia's most vibrant cities, HBX 2027 brings together dancers, instructors, and DJs from across Asia and beyond for three nights of social dancing and unforgettable connection.

From Friday night opening socials to Sunday evening competitions and late-night dancing at Hong Mai Ballroom — every moment of HBX is designed with you in mind.

Instructors, DJs, and dancers gathered from across Asia and the world.

Welcoming & Safe

A space built on respect, consent, and the joy of dance.

What to Expect

  • A blues dance workshop weekend open to any and all experience levels — complete beginners are very welcome
  • International professional blues dance instructors teaching lessons catered towards social dancing
  • Community events including eating together, time to explore the amazing city of Hanoi, and plenty of social dancing until 2am
  • Some of the best DJs in Asia spinning the finest blues dance tunes all weekend long
  • Exciting competitions designed to inspire dancers and celebrate the joy of the dance
  • Blues dance parties going until 2am every night
  • Amazing competitions, high quality music from top-tier DJs, and productive classes that will genuinely help your dancing
  • An incredibly good time with great people in one of Asia's most vibrant cities

Show Your Moves

Competitions

Four competitions across the weekend — 6 couple finalists each, 1.5-minute spotlight finals. Competition entry is priced separately from your event pass.

🌱

Newcomer Mix & Match

A welcoming entry point for newer dancers — celebrate your progress and meet new partners.

When: Friday Night
Eligibility: Never finaled · Under 2 years blues experience
Format: Mix & Match
Finals: 6 couples · 1.5 min spotlight
⚡

Open Fast Blues M&M

Energy, rhythm, and playfulness take center stage. Bring your best boogie and bounce.

When: Saturday Afternoon
Eligibility: Open to all
Format: Mix & Match
Finals: 6 couples · 1.5 min spotlight
💑

Open Strictly

Compete with your own partner and show your chemistry and connection on the floor.

When: Saturday Late Night
Eligibility: Open to all
Format: Strictly (fixed partner)
Finals: 6 couples · 1.5 min spotlight
🌙

Open Slow Blues M&M

Connection, groove, and musicality front and center. Show the judges your slow blues feeling.

When: Sunday Evening
Eligibility: Open to all
Format: Mix & Match
Finals: 6 couples · 1.5 min spotlight

🎓 Level Up with Private Lessons

HBX has been structured so that our instructors and special guests have free time built into the weekend — and one of the best ways to take your dancing to the next level is a private lesson. We highly encourage you to book private time with both our instructors and special guests. Private lesson costs are not included in the Full Pass or Party Pass — they are arranged and paid directly to the instructor or special guest. Reach out early, spots fill fast!
