Our Story

What Is HBX?

Blues Dance comes to Hanoi

The Hanoi Blues Experience (HBX) is a weekend blues dance event built around community, connection, and the deep feeling of the blues. Set against the backdrop of one of Southeast Asia's most vibrant cities, HBX 2027 brings together dancers, instructors, and DJs from across Asia and beyond for three nights of social dancing, live music, and unforgettable connection.

From Friday night socials with a live jazz band at Blake's House of Jazz, to Saturday afternoon workshops and competitions at Hong Mai Ballroom, to a Sunday community picnic on Hanoi Lake — every moment of HBX is designed with you in mind.

📅 Dates to note: HBX takes place January 23–25, 2027 — the last weekend of January, alongside our friends at Breezy Balboa. Plan to attend both!

Competitions

Four competitions — prelims Saturday at 5pm, all finals Saturday evening. Competition DJ: Roman.

🌱

Newcomer Mix & Match

For dancers who have never finaled in any competition. A welcoming, low-pressure entry point to competing — celebrate your progress and meet new partners on the floor.

Eligibility: Never finaled in any competition
Format: Mix & Match (rotating partners)
Prelims: Sat 17:00 · DJ: Roman
Finals: Sat evening
🌙

Open Slow Blues M&M

Where connection, groove, and musicality take center stage. Open to all levels — show the judges your slow blues feeling.

Eligibility: Open to all
Format: Mix & Match (rotating partners)
Prelims: Sat 17:00 · DJ: Roman
Finals: Sat evening
⚡

Open Fast Blues M&M

Turn up the tempo. Fast blues rewards energy, rhythm, and playfulness. Open to all — bring your best boogie and bounce.

Eligibility: Open to all
Format: Mix & Match (rotating partners)
Prelims: Sat 17:00 · DJ: Roman
Finals: Sat evening
💑

Open Strictly

Compete with your own partner. A strictly blues format for established couples to show their chemistry and connection on the floor.

Eligibility: Open to all partnerships
Format: Strictly (fixed partner)
Prelims: Sat 17:00 · DJ: Roman
Finals: Sat evening
🎧 All competition prelims and finals are spun by Roman — our dedicated competition DJ for HBX 2027.

🎛️ DJ Tips for Blues Socials

Tempo Awareness

Blues dancing spans ~60–160 BPM. Slow blues is 60–90, medium 90–120, fast blues above 120. Mix with intention and awareness of where the floor is.

Read the Room

Watch the dancers, not your screen. If the floor empties, adjust. Your job is to keep people dancing — not to play your personal favorites.

Set Arc

Open light, build through the middle, peak two-thirds in, then bring it back for a graceful close. A good set has a story from first to last song.

Music Discovery

Listen widely — blues, R&B, soul, gospel, boogie. The best blues DJs have deep libraries and bring surprises that still feel like blues.

Competition DJing

Choose tracks with clear rhythmic cues, variety in tempo, and enough length for judges. Avoid overly complex arrangements that distract from the dancing.

Start Small

DJ community socials or practice sessions first. Volunteer, get feedback, and build your ear before stepping up to bigger events.
